# Logo

Rocky Start has a primary lockup (the mountain mark beside the name), a one-colour survey seal, and an "RS" mark for small sizes.

## Primary lockup: mountain range

- A range of three peaks, outlined in ink, with a thin pencil line of a more distant range behind. The near range is filled with the brand's ruled marker strata (ochre, rust, ink, slate, pine), tilted slightly. The colour was put in by hand after the outline was stamped, one wide marker stroke at a time, like the cover strata. Each stroke has its own length: a slanted chisel start with a small pool of ink, and an uneven lift-off. Ends overshoot the slope in places and stop just short in others, and the whole fill sits a hair off-register. Beside it, "Rocky Start" is set in Alegreya SC, with "EXPLORATION SERVICES" in Courier Prime 700 capitals underneath, tracked out.
- Use it on report covers, the website header, proposals and signage.
- Use the light lockup (`logos/rocky-start-lockup-light.svg`) on `paper` and the dark lockup (`rocky-start-lockup-dark.svg`) on dark `paper`. The mark alone is in `rocky-start-mark-light.svg` / `-dark.svg`.
- The bottom of the mark's ground line sits on the baseline of the "EXPLORATION SERVICES" line.
- Leave clear space around it of at least half the mark's height.
- Minimum size is a 40px-tall mark (12 mm in print). Below that, switch to the RS mark.

## Survey seal

- A round, one-colour `rust` seal with a double ring. "Rocky Start" runs around the top in Alegreya SC bold, and "EXPLORATION SERVICES" runs around the bottom in Courier Prime capitals, with a dot at either side. The same three-peak range sits in the centre: a bold near range, a thin distant range behind it, and a ground line. A few faint rust bands across the near range echo the marker strata, coloured in by hand the same way: separate strokes of different lengths, slightly past the lines in places. On a rubber stamp the bands may print solid or drop out; the outlines carry the picture either way.
- Use it on letterhead, report back covers, core-box labels, sample bags, invoices and as a watermark.
- Keep it at least 24 mm (about 90px) across, so the lettering around the rim stays readable. Below that, use the RS mark.

## RS mark

- "RS" in Alegreya SC bold over a double strata underline (a heavy rule and a light one), inside a hand-ruled square, in one colour.
- Use it for favicons, social avatars, app icons and anything under 40px.

Both the seal and the RS mark can be reproduced in any single ink (rust, ink, or paper on a dark ground), so they work for rubber stamps, embossing and one-colour print.

## Don'ts

- Don't recolour the strata, add shadows or outlines, or set the name in any face other than Alegreya SC.
- Don't redraw the mark. Its hand-drawn variation is fixed, so use the files.
- Don't place the full-colour mark on photos or busy backgrounds. Use the seal in one colour instead.

## Files

- Finished SVG files of the lockup, mark, seal and RS mark, in light and dark versions, are in the `logos/` folder. Their lettering is converted to shapes, so they need no fonts installed.
- The font files (Alegreya SC 500 and 700, Courier Prime 400 and 700) are in `fonts/`, with their open font licences. Kalam is loaded from Google Fonts.
